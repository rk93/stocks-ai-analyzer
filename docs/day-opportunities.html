How the signal works

Rewards: VWAP reclaim/hold, opening-range breakout, 1.4x–2x+ recent bar volume, positive 15-minute momentum, support proximity, positive 1-month trend and enough ATR to support an intraday move. It penalises names already stretched far above VWAP or already up sharply, because the goal is to find the move early rather than chase it.

ENTRY TRIGGERED requires score ≥68, unusual volume, price no more than 2.5% above VWAP, and either a VWAP reclaim or opening-range breakout. WATCH means conditions are developing. NO TRADE is a valid outcome.
